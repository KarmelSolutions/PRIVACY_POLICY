# Kaman POS Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 8, 2026
**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

Kaman POS ("Kaman," "we," "us," or "our") is a point-of-sale application for restaurant staff. This policy explains how information is handled when a restaurant uses the Kaman POS app and its related services. It applies to the Android and iOS app. The restaurant is responsible for the customer and business information its staff enter into the service; Kaman provides the software and related support.

## 1. Information processed

Depending on the features used, the app processes:

- **Staff and account information:** restaurant identifier, staff name, email address, role, sign-in credentials, authentication token, and a device identifier generated by the app.
- **Restaurant operations:** menu items, orders, tables, order notes, customer history, prices, discounts, receipts, payment method, payment amount, and transaction status. If staff enter them, an order may include a customer's name, phone number, and delivery address.
- **Device and support information:** app version, platform, device name or identifier, language, printer configuration and status, diagnostic logs, support messages, and information supplied in a support ticket. Some diagnostic information is sent to Kaman's support service when the restaurant has enabled its support tools.
- **Local settings and cached data:** sign-in session, restaurant catalog, order sync data, images used by the catalog, printer settings, and display preferences stored on the device to operate the app and support interrupted connectivity.
- **Network information:** IP address and ordinary request details that may be recorded by the restaurant service or support server when the app connects to them.

The app does not request access to the device's precise location, contacts, photo library, camera, or microphone on Android or iOS. It does not read SMS messages. A customer's address or location description may still be entered manually by staff as part of an order.

## 2. How information is used

We use information to authenticate staff, display and synchronize restaurant data, process orders and payments, connect to receipt and kitchen printers, deliver customer messages requested by staff through the restaurant service, provide support, diagnose failures, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. Payment card processing, when used, is handled through the restaurant's configured payment service; Kaman POS records payment method, amount, and status needed for the order. Staff should not enter full card numbers into order notes or support messages.

We do not sell personal information. Kaman POS does not contain a third-party advertising network or track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

## 3. Sharing and service providers

Order and staff information is transmitted to the restaurant's Kaman service and is available to authorized restaurant personnel according to their access. Information may also be shared with:

- hosting and infrastructure providers that operate the restaurant and Kaman support services;
- the restaurant's configured payment or messaging providers when staff use those features;
- OpenAI when the optional AI support chat is used, to process the conversation and information provided to that feature; and
- professional advisers or authorities when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or the service.

We limit service-provider access to what is needed for the relevant service. A restaurant may have its own privacy notice for information it collects from its customers.

## 4. Local network, storage, and security

The app may use the restaurant's local network to discover or connect to receipt and kitchen printers. Printer addresses and settings are stored on the device. The app also stores session and operational cache files locally. Access to a device with an active session may expose restaurant data, so restaurant operators should secure their devices and staff accounts.

Connections to the restaurant and Kaman support services use HTTPS. We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Restaurant records are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and for applicable accounting, legal, security, and dispute-resolution requirements. Support tickets and diagnostics are kept only as needed for support and those purposes. Different records may have different retention periods.

Signing out removes the active authentication token from the app's local session. Cached restaurant data and some local settings may remain on the device until cleared through the app or device controls, or until the app is removed. Signing out does **not** by itself delete the staff account or the restaurant's server records.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Restaurant administrators control which staff can access the service and which optional support features are enabled. Staff can avoid adding optional customer details to an order when they are not needed. Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information and may object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us using the details below.

## 7. International processing

Service providers may process information outside the country where a restaurant or user is located. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 8. Children

Kaman POS is a business tool for restaurant staff and is not directed to children. Staff should avoid entering children's personal information unless it is necessary for a restaurant order and permitted by applicable law.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The date above will be revised when we publish an update. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
