# K27 Academy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

K27 Academy ("K27," "we," "us," or "our") provides a family-facing application for managing children’s football academy activities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, when it is shared, how long it is retained, and the choices available to parents and legal guardians.

This policy applies to the K27 mobile application and the services that support it. The application is intended to be used by a child’s parent or legal guardian. It is not intended for children to create or independently manage accounts.

## 1. Information we collect

Depending on the features used, we may process:

- **Guardian account information:** name, mobile number, additional phone number, email address, address, account identifier, and authentication records.
- **Child information:** name, date of birth, gender, identification number, residential area, uniform size, training group, player status, notes, photo, and attachments supplied to the academy.
- **Academy records:** program registrations, training dates and locations, attendance, subscriptions, fees, balances, payment amounts, payment dates, payment status, and payment method records.
- **Posts and media:** post interactions such as likes, posts in which a child is tagged, media-consent status, and images or videos made available by the academy.
- **Notification information:** a push-notification token, selected app language, notification preference, and notification delivery or read status when notifications are enabled.
- **Technical and security information:** IP address, request time, response status, device or operating-system information made available through standard network requests, security events, and diagnostic errors. Application logs are designed to avoid recording authentication tokens, one-time codes, or full private records.
- **Device preferences:** language, theme, onboarding completion, and reminder settings stored locally on the device. The authentication token is stored using secure device storage.

We receive information directly from the guardian, from authorized academy staff, through use of the application, and from the service providers described below.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, and protect accounts using one-time verification codes;
- link a guardian with the correct children and prevent unauthorized access;
- manage child registration, training programs, schedules, attendance, subscriptions, fees, and payment records;
- display academy announcements, private family posts, and content in which a child is tagged;
- schedule on-device training reminders and send opted-in push notifications;
- provide customer support and respond to privacy requests;
- prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and technical failures;
- maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability and accessibility of the service; and
- comply with legal, accounting, safeguarding, and dispute-resolution obligations.

We do **not** sell personal information. The app contains no third-party advertising network and does not track users across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

## 3. Legal grounds

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information to provide the service requested by the guardian, to meet contractual and legal obligations, to protect children and the security of the service, and on the basis of consent where consent is appropriate, including optional child-media consent and optional notifications. Consent can be withdrawn for future processing without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

## 4. Children’s privacy and media

The service processes children’s information under the direction of a parent or legal guardian and authorized academy personnel. Guardians must provide accurate information and only information they are authorized to provide.

A child’s image or identifying tag may be included in restricted academy posts only when the academy has recorded the relevant media consent. A guardian can withdraw that consent by contacting the academy. Withdrawal applies to future use; we will also assess reasonable removal requests for existing material. Child records and private media are restricted to authorized accounts and academy personnel with a work-related need.

## 5. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed with:

- **Authorized K27 Academy personnel** who manage registration, attendance, training, payments, support, and approved communications.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** that store or transmit application data for us.
- **Meta/WhatsApp Cloud API**, which receives the destination phone number and message-delivery information needed to send a one-time verification code.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging**, which receives a device token and message-delivery information when push notifications are enabled. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in the application.
- **Professional advisers, authorities, or other parties** when disclosure is required by law, needed to protect a child or another person, or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Providers acting on our behalf are expected to use appropriate safeguards and process information only for the service they provide. We do not authorize service providers to use K27 family data for their own advertising.

## 6. Permissions and device access

The app may request notification permission so it can deliver training and academy alerts. It may request limited photo or storage access only when a user chooses to save an academy image or video to the device. On-device training reminders use local notification scheduling. Permissions can be denied or revoked in device settings; related features may then be unavailable.

## 7. Retention and deletion

We retain guardian and child records while the family has an active relationship with K27 Academy and afterward only for periods reasonably required by legal, accounting, safeguarding, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution obligations. Specific records may therefore have different retention periods.

- One-time verification records expire after five minutes and are removed after successful use; expired records are periodically cleared.
- Mobile access tokens expire after 30 days and are revoked when the user signs out.
- Push tokens are removed when notification registration is disabled, on supported sign-out flows, or when the associated account is deleted.
- Local preferences remain on the device until cleared by the user, app removal, or operating-system controls.

Users can initiate permanent account deletion from **Account → Security and privacy → Delete account** in the app. A verified deletion removes the account and associated child records, subject to information we must retain under applicable law. A deletion request can also be submitted using the contact details below. We may verify the requester’s identity and guardianship before acting. Information that must be retained will be isolated, access-restricted, and deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

## 8. Security

We use role-based access controls, authenticated private-media endpoints, rate limits, expiring access tokens, secure device storage, and encrypted HTTPS transport in production. We limit access to people who need information for academy operations. No system can guarantee absolute security, so we review safeguards and respond to suspected incidents as appropriate.

## 9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, a guardian may request access to, a copy of, correction of, restriction of, or deletion of personal information, and may object to certain processing or withdraw consent. Profile information can be reviewed or updated in the app. Notifications can be disabled in the app or device settings. Media saved to a device can be deleted from the device gallery.

To submit a request, email [support@karmelplus.com](mailto:support@karmelplus.com?subject=K27%20privacy%20request) with the phone number linked to the account and the type of request. Do not send identification documents or other sensitive information unless we specifically request them through a secure channel.

## 10. International processing

Our infrastructure, messaging, and notification providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country. Where required, we use appropriate contractual and technical safeguards and limit transfers to the information necessary to provide the service.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application, our data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will show a revised effective date. We will provide an appropriate in-app or other notice before a material change takes effect when required.
